GaussManinResurgence.TaylorToPade — Method
TaylorToPade(a, m, n)

Compute the [m/n] Padé approximant from Taylor coefficients a, where a[k+1] is the coefficient of x^k.

Returns (num, den) where

  • num are numerator coefficients in increasing order
  • den are denominator coefficients in increasing order, with den[1] == 1
