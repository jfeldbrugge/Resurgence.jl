GaussManinResurgence.frobenius_solution — Method

Saddle solution Φ̂j(z) = (z - σj)^ρ Σk ck (z - σj)^k with c0 = v, Rj v = ρ v (principal branch), from the Frobenius recursion ((ρ + k) I - Rj) ck = Σ{m<k} Am c{k-1-m}. Needs |z - σ_j| < distance to the other letters, and ρ not a negative integer.

GaussManinResurgence.keyhole_paths — Method

Keyhole paths (γ₊, γ₋) from a = σi + ri e to b = σj + rj e, e = (σj - σi)/|σj - σi|. Singular points σk on (or close to) the segment σi → σj are avoided by half-circles on the left (side = +1) or right (side = -1) of the directed line; γ₊ and γ₋ differ only around σj.

GaussManinResurgence.leading_vector — Method

Image of the (rank-one) residue R, normalised to unit first entry: the leading vector v with R v = tr(R) v. Taken from the SVD rather than an eigen-decomposition, which is ill-conditioned (≈ √eps) when R is nilpotent (tr R = 0).

GaussManinResurgence.local_singular — Method

Local solution at the singular point σj: Lj(z) = H(z-σj) exp(xj log(z-σj)), H holomorphic with H(0) = 1 (principal branch of log). Needs |z-σj| < dist to other letters. Solves k hk - [xj, hk] = Σm Am h{k-1-m} order by order.

GaussManinResurgence.stokes_constant — Method

Stokes constant K{j←i} for the Laplace contour of saddle i crossing the Borel singularity σj, from the keyhole contour around σj. vs[a] is the leading vector of Φ̂a; by default it is T0[a] times the eigenvector of Ra with unit first entry. For integer Stokes constants, T0[a] should be the normalisation of the asymptotic series of saddle a, T0[a] = 1/√det H(xa), ordered like Σ. Returns (K, S, relresidual) with S = U₋⁻¹U₊ the keyhole holonomy and relresidual the part of the jump not proportional to Φ̂_j (should be ≈ 0).

method = :hyperlog evaluates the free hyperlogarithm series truncated at weight N (giving access to the weight expansion); method = :matrix transports the matrices directly, with no weight truncation (N is then ignored). Use :matrix for more than a few saddles.

N is either an integer (used as given) or :auto. With :auto (hyperlog only) the smallest N with certified truncation error |KN - K| < 1/2 is used (`sufficientN, keywordsNmax,sharp,aposteriori), so thatround(K)is exact when the Stokes constants are integers. The bound assumes nilpotent residues (ρ = 0, the package convention). If it certifies no N ≤ Nmax, or the certified N would need more thanmaxwordswords (about n^N for n letters), it falls back tostokesconstantincrement` within that budget.

GaussManinResurgence.stokes_constant_increment — Method

Practical stopping rule (not a certificate): increase N from Nmin until KN and K{N+1} round to the same integer and differ by less than tol. Returns ((K, S, residual), N).

GaussManinResurgence.trace_contributions — Method

Weight-resolved tr((Ti - I)(Tj - I)): entry r-1 is the total-weight-r contribution Σ{p=1}^{r-1} tr(Di[p+1] Dj[r-p+1]), r = 2..N, with Di the weight pieces of Ti (e.g. from `monodromypieces`). The leading entry is (2πi)² tr(Ri Rj).

GaussManinResurgence.truncation_bound — Method

Certified bound N ↦ B(N) ≥ |KN - K{j←i}| for the hyperlogarithm back-end truncated at weight N, valid for nilpotent residues (ρ = 0). sharp uses ‖Σs Rs/(t - σs)‖ instead of Σs ‖Rs‖/|t - σs|; a_posteriori takes ‖Φ̂a‖, ‖Φ̂b‖, ‖U₊ - U₋‖ and |K| from the matrix back-end instead of a-priori bounds.
